Abode OS

The operating system for a real estate brokerage

A brokerage does not run on a calendar. It runs on deal files — where one missing disclosure holds a closing, where the commission splits three ways before it reaches anyone, and where the managing broker is on the hook for every agent's paperwork. Abode OS is built around that: the file, the gate, and the money, with every seat seeing exactly its own book.

The compliance gate

A transaction cannot clear to close until every file document is in and reviewed. Kicked-back documents sit on the home screen until somebody fixes them.

One system, three scopes

An agent sees only their own book. A team lead sees the team. The managing broker sees the whole firm's transactions, compliance log and financials — no app-switching.

A CRM that scores behavior

Leads ranked on what they actually do, with the hot ones to call first. Lead gen and the IDX sites feed it; nothing is retyped.

Commissions, splits and caps

The commission spine computes every split and every cap, and the company dollar room shows what the brokerage keeps and what agents owe in desk fees.

Listings that watch the clock

Active listings against days on market, so the one past sixty days is a line on the home screen, not a surprise at the sales meeting.

No cut of your commissions

One flat license for the brokerage. It does not cost more when you add an agent, and it never takes a slice of a closing.
