Abode OS is the operating system for a real estate brokerage — lead gen and the IDX sites, a behavior-scored CRM, MLS-fed listings, the transaction & broker-compliance gate, the commission money spine with splits and caps, analytics, recruiting, and a ten-department agent org routing underneath all of it.

Multi-tier by design. An agent sees only their own book. A team lead sees the team. The managing broker sees the whole firm's transactions, compliance log and financials. One system, three scopes — no app-switching, and no tuition-style cut of your commissions.
This is the real hub, not a slideshow. It runs entirely in your browser. Everything you type stays in this tab, nothing is uploaded, and the floor resets when you leave. Nothing here sends, spends, publishes or moves money — anything that would is staged on the Approval Desk instead.
